How it works and how it was tested

Two kinds of evidence. Real maps: OpenStreetMap and Microsoft footprints of T. Nagar, with 300 pairs checked by hand against satellite imagery. Synthetic stress tests: real footprints with planted errors, so every match can be scored exactly.
